## About Me

I'm an AI researcher and engineer with a background in **Electrical Engineering** and a Master's in **Sound Engineering** (Signal Processing & AI).

My journey started with AVR microcontrollers, building an engineering calculator that could solve 5x5 complex equations, and designing a real-world smart home system during the COVID era using WeMos D1.

Today, I work at the intersection of **speech AI, deep learning, and DSP** — building Text-to-Speech systems and generative speech technologies. I'm equally comfortable talking about beamforming on spherical microphone arrays and embedded hardware design — and that's exactly the perspective I bring.

I believe the best AI systems are built by engineers who understand both the **theory** and the **hardware**.
