## About Me

I'm an AI researcher and engineer with an academic background in Electrical Engineering and a Master's degree in Sound Engineering, specializing in Signal Processing.

My work lies at the intersection of **signal processing, machine learning, and intelligent systems**, with a primary focus on speech and audio technologies.

I have worked on speech synthesis, including Emotional TTS and Zero-Shot TTS, as well as speech processing, phonetic modeling, and deep learning-based audio systems. My engineering background also includes embedded systems, acoustics, and electrical engineering.

Beyond speech AI, I have research interests in intelligent energy systems, optimization, and the application of AI to engineering problems.

## Selected Projects

### Speech & Audio Intelligence

- 🔤 **Persian G2P:** Grapheme-to-Phoneme conversion for Persian, including phonetic modeling, homograph disambiguation, and Ezafe handling.
- 🎙️ **Emotional & Zero-Shot TTS:** Research and development of expressive and speaker-adaptive speech synthesis systems.
- 🗣️ **Far-field Speech Recognition:** Research on distant speech recognition using microphone arrays and advanced signal processing.
- 🔊 **Spherical Microphone Array Beamforming:** Investigation of spatial audio processing and beamforming techniques for speech enhancement and recognition.

### Embedded Systems & Engineering

- 🧮 **Engineering Calculator:** An AVR-based engineering calculator designed to solve complex systems of up to five equations and five unknowns.
- 🏠 **Smart Home / IoT:** An embedded smart home prototype using WeMos D1, environmental sensors, relays, and remote mobile control.

### Intelligent Energy Systems

- **HyLL:** A hybrid LSTM and Large Language Model-based recommendation system for energy management and consumption optimization in smart homes.
